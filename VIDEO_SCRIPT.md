Everything except the DEMO block can be recorded now (ElevenLabs or voice) since it doesn't depend on live Bob output.

1. Record/generate all narration blocks above **except the DEMO block** now — total non-demo narration should land under 55 seconds combined, leaving the demo room to breathe past 90 seconds without blowing the 3:00 cap.
2. Lay each audio clip against its matching slide in your video editor, in order — the timecodes are a planning guide, not exact cut points.
3. On Sept 25/26, after your first real Bob session, fill in the DEMO block's bracketed placeholders, record that narration last, and drop it in.
4. **Final pass: time the whole assembled video and confirm it does not exceed 3:00** — trim the demo recording (not the narration) if you're over, since the demo is the one part judges are told to expect at length.
