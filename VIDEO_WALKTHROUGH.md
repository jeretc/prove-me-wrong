# Video Production Walkthrough — "Prove Me Wrong"

A step-by-step order of operations for producing the submission video. Follow top to bottom — don't worry about exact timing, just do each step and move to the next.

**⚠️ Hard cap confirmed 2026-09-25: the video must not exceed 3:00 total — judges will not watch past that. At least 90 seconds must be the live demo (Step 4/6 below).** The script in VIDEO_SCRIPT.md is already budgeted to this; don't pad the non-demo clips beyond what's written there.

## Before Sept 25 (can do now)

**Step 1 — Build the slides**
Turn PRESENTATION.md's 10 slides into an actual slide deck (Google Slides, PowerPoint, Canva — whatever you're comfortable with). Just the 9 non-demo slides for now; slide 7 (live demo) stays a placeholder/title card like "Live Demo" until you have real footage.

**Step 2 — Record the 5 non-demo narration clips**
Open VIDEO_SCRIPT.md. For each block EXCEPT the demo one, record/generate the narration as its own separate audio file — keep these short, per the script (combined they should land under ~55 seconds so the demo has room to breathe). Name them in order:
- `01-mia-story.mp3` (0:00-0:20)
- `02-stakes.mp3` (0:20-0:35)
- `03-insight-solution.mp3` (0:35-0:55)
- `04-business-originality.mp3` (2:35-2:50, goes AFTER the demo)
- `05-close.mp3` (2:50-3:00)
(Note: "why Bob 2.0" is no longer a separate clip — it's folded into the end of the demo narration itself, to fit the cap.)

**Step 3 — Rough-assemble what you have**
In your video editor, place clips 1-3 in order, each with its matching slide underneath. Leave a gap for the demo, then clips 4-5 after it. You'll now have a "video with a hole in it" — that's expected and fine.

## On Sept 25/26 (once Bob 2.0 access opens)

**Step 4 — Do the real demo, screen-recorded**
Run your actual onboarding-interview session against Axios with screen recording on (OBS Studio, or Windows' built-in Xbox Game Bar capture / Win+Alt+R). Do one full pass: question → your guess → Bob's correction+citation → adaptive follow-up → summary screen. This is also when you capture the Bob task-session screenshots per BOB_CAPTURE_PLAN.md (the exact official procedure — Tasks list → task header → screenshot — is different from what we originally guessed, so use that file, not memory of an older version) — same session covers both needs.

**Step 5 — Write and record the demo narration**
Now that you've seen exactly what happened on screen, fill in VIDEO_SCRIPT.md's demo placeholder with the real details (the actual question, your actual guess, Bob's actual correction, plus the "why Bob 2.0" closing line) and record that narration clip last — `demo.mp3`.

**Step 6 — Drop the demo into the hole**
Insert the screen recording + its narration into the gap you left in Step 3. Aim for the demo alone to run **at least 90 seconds** (it's a hard requirement) but keep the whole video under 3:00 total — trim the raw screen recording to one clean guess-correction-summary cycle, not the whole session.

**Step 7 — Final watch-through and export**
Play the whole thing start to finish once and **time it** — it must not exceed 3:00, and the demo portion must be at least 90 seconds. Check: does each clip's audio roughly match its visual, does it end cleanly on the close slide. Export and upload wherever the submission form wants it.

## The short version
Slides + 5 narration clips + rough-assemble → (wait for kickoff) → record real demo + its narration (with "why Bob" folded in) → drop into the gap → time-check against the 3:00 cap → export.
