# Video Production Walkthrough — "Prove Me Wrong"

A step-by-step order of operations for producing the submission video. Follow top to bottom — don't worry about exact timing, just do each step and move to the next.

## Before Sept 25 (can do now)

**Step 1 — Build the slides**
Turn PRESENTATION.md's 10 slides into an actual slide deck (Google Slides, PowerPoint, Canva — whatever you're comfortable with). Just the 9 non-demo slides for now; slide 7 (live demo) stays a placeholder/title card like "Live Demo" until you have real footage.

**Step 2 — Record the 6 non-demo narration clips**
Open VIDEO_SCRIPT.md. For each block EXCEPT the demo one, record/generate the narration as its own separate audio file. Name them in order so you don't lose track:
- `01-mia-story.mp3`
- `02-stats.mp3`
- `03-insight-solution.mp3`
- `04-why-bob.mp3`
- `05-business-value.mp3`
- `06-close.mp3`
(The demo clip becomes `04b` or similar once you record it later — insert it between "insight-solution" and "why-bob".)

**Step 3 — Rough-assemble what you have**
In your video editor, place slides 1-6, 8-10 in order, each with its matching audio clip underneath, trimmed to fit. Leave a blank/title-card gap where the demo goes. You'll now have a "video with a hole in it" — that's expected and fine.

## On Sept 25/26 (once Bob 2.0 access opens)

**Step 4 — Do the real demo, screen-recorded**
Run your actual onboarding-interview session against Axios with screen recording on (OBS Studio, or Windows' built-in Xbox Game Bar capture / Win+Alt+R). Do one full pass: question → your guess → Bob's correction+citation → adaptive follow-up → summary screen. This is also when you capture the Bob-session screenshots for BOB_CAPTURE_PLAN.md — same session covers both needs.

**Step 5 — Write and record the demo narration**
Now that you've seen exactly what happened on screen, fill in VIDEO_SCRIPT.md's demo placeholder with the real details (the actual question, your actual guess, Bob's actual correction) and record that narration clip last — `04-demo.mp3` (or whatever slots between the insight and why-Bob clips).

**Step 6 — Drop the demo into the hole**
Insert the screen recording + its narration into the gap you left in Step 3. Trim the screen recording so it's not too long — a single clean guess-correction-summary cycle is enough, you don't need the whole session.

**Step 7 — Final watch-through and export**
Play the whole thing start to finish once. Check: does each clip's audio roughly match its visual, is the demo the right length (not rushed, not dragging), does it end cleanly on the close slide. Export and upload wherever the submission form wants it.

## The short version
Slides + 6 narration clips + rough-assemble → (wait for kickoff) → record real demo + its narration → drop into the gap → final check → export. You're never doing timestamp math — you're just filling in blocks in order.
